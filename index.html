Consumer Copilot: where the next growth comes from

Outside-in analysis by Omsri Yarram, from public filings. October 2026.

Both consumer revenue engines are slowing at the same time

Search ad growth halved in a year. Consumer subscription growth was lifted by the Copilot price increase, which is now wearing off. This analysis sizes three ways for consumer Copilot to restore growth, and names the two numbers that decide between them.

Suggested path: scale ads in free Copilot first, test a low-price AI plan in a few markets with a pass mark of about 1.2% paid conversion, and keep the Premium upsell running.

The three options

Three-year value is the net present value of gross profit, FY27 to FY29, at a 9% discount rate. Each option is sized on its own. Change the assumptions on the Options model tab.

What would change the answer

  1. Ads fall behind the Premium upsell if they earn under about $2 per user a year by FY29, or if, at $2, more than roughly 35 to 40% of that money would have come through Bing anyway. At $3 they stay ahead even if half comes from Bing.
  2. The low-price plan only beats the Premium upsell if more than about 1.2% of free Copilot users pay for it by FY29.

Both can be measured before committing: an ads holdout group for the first, a two-market price test for the second.
